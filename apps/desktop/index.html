Devices

Connect your keyboard by USB and switch it on. EkidongoMIDI remembers your choice and connects by itself next time.

Playing

Your setups

Save your registration buttons and set lists to a file, for a backup or to use on another computer (for example the church keyboard).

Importing replaces your 8 registration buttons and adds the file's set lists.

About

EkidongoMIDI

Free software under the GNU GPL v3. The style library is not covered by the GPL.

"Live Drums" styles use drum performances from the Groove MIDI Dataset by Google LLC, licensed under CC BY 4.0 (magenta.tensorflow.org/datasets/groove). Selected bars were cut and notes mapped to General MIDI.

Error reports, if any, are sent without personal data so problems can be fixed.
